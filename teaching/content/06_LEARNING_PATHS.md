# Learning paths
## How a user of any age moves through the library

The twelve rooms are the same in every book.
Paths are suggested orders so nobody has to stare at 66 doors and freeze.

---

## Path A — “I have never really read this”
Time: 15–20 minutes a sitting, two sittings a week.
1. What kind of library is this? (Canon Room, short)
2. How a book travels (Ink primer)
3. Mark (a Gospel first — meet Jesus)
4. Genesis 12–50 only on the first pass (the family), then 1–11
5. Exodus 1–15 (rescue), then 19–20 (the words)
6. Jonah
7. Luke 15 (as a room inside Luke once Luke is built)
8. Romans 8 and 12 (then the whole letter)
9. Psalm 23, 51, 139
10. Revelation 1–3 and 21–22 only, then the rest if they want

Rule: Gospels before Romans. Rescue before law. Letters to churches before systems.

---

## Path B — “I grew up in church and still feel lost”
1. How to read without getting lost
2. Mark in one sitting
3. Genesis full module, including Two Chairs on 1–2
4. Exodus full module
5. 1 Samuel 16 – 2 Samuel 12 (David, when those modules exist)
6. Isaiah door + servant songs (once Isaiah is gold)
7. Romans full module
8. Revelation full module with all four chairs
9. Job (when gold) — for the problem of speeches that are too sure

---

## Path C — “I want the fight and the footnotes”
Summit layer default.
Order by genre literacy:
1. Ink primer + Canon Room
2. Genesis (authorship, origins, Ink)
3. Jonah (genre)
4. Isaiah (when gold — one prophet or more)
5. Daniel (when gold — 6th century court tale vs. 2nd century resistance writing)
6. Mark (ending)
7. Romans (righteousness, works of the law)
8. Revelation (four chairs)
9. Then fill prophets and letters

---

## Path D — Child / family
Path layer only. Hard Ground behind a door.
Map + Campfire + three remember-items.
Suggested family nights:
- Jonah and a map to Nineveh
- Mark 4–5 (storm, a girl raised)
- Exodus 12–14 (meal and sea)
- Psalm as a prayer they can copy
- Revelation 21 as a city of healing, not beasts first

---

## Path E — “Show me Jesus in the library”
Not allegory-on-everything. Just the books that the NT itself leans on hardest.
Mark → Genesis 12 → Exodus 12–15 and 33–34 → Psalm 22 → Isaiah door → Jonah → Romans → Hebrews (when gold) → Revelation 4–5.

---

## A year in rooms (optional later)
Fifty-two weeks, one book or book-section a week, always in this shape:
Sunday: Door + Campfire  
Midweek: Map + Walkthrough  
Saturday: One Chair or one Ink note + Jesus thread

Skip the guilt streak. Missed weeks do not reset a person. They reset a bookmark.
