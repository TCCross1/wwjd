## How to read a line

`Door` — first image, no holy-card face unless the book is a Gospel and even then no glowing European Jesus.  
`Campfire` — three tap-stills.  
`Object` — tray piece from `12_OBJECT_PER_BOOK.md`.

All prompts assume: cinematic quiet light, historically careful, caption later, almost no type on the image.

## Torah

**Genesis**  
Door: walled Middle Bronze city at dusk, two travelers and a well outside the gate.  
Campfire: a garden river / a boat on dark water / a father and sons on a road toward Egypt.  
Object: city gate and rampart.

**Exodus**  
Door: mud-brick yard, wooden molds, heat, an overseer in the middle distance.  
Campfire: a riverbasket in reeds / a sea wall of water / a tent with fire at the center of a camp.  
Object: brick mold.

**Leviticus**  
Door: four-horned limestone altar, smoke thin in morning air, courtyard stones.  
Campfire: washbasin / priest’s hands over a bird / a camp arranged around a holy tent.  
Object: four-horned altar.

**Numbers**  
Door: a people counted in a wilderness basin, tents in rings, a clouded peak far off.  
Campfire: silver trumpets / bronze snake lifted / Jordan glittering from a ridge.  
Object: Ketef Hinnom-class silver scroll.

**Deuteronomy**  
Door: an old man speaking on a Moab ridge, the river and the land in front of him.  
Campfire: a doorpost / a small box on a forehead in humble scale / two ways, life and death, as two roads.  
Object: tefillin / Shema on a door.

**Esther**

## Major prophets

**Isaiah**  
Door: a temple threshold and smoke, a man covering his mouth.  
Campfire: a city shut like a birdcage / a highway in the wilderness / a servant figure, not a portrait.  
Object: Great Isaiah Scroll + Sennacherib prism class.

**Jeremiah**  
Door: a gate officer with an ostracon, hills beyond, one signal dark.  
Campfire: a yoke / a burned scroll / a bought field.  
Object: Lachish letter.

**Lamentations**  
Door: ash on Jerusalem stone, a woman seated, no spectacle.  
Campfire: empty streets / a child and a crust / a gate that does not close well.  
Object: 586 burn-layer beam and jar.

**Ezekiel**  
Door: a canal, date palms, a priest sitting among packed bags.  
Campfire: wheels in a storm / a valley of bones as landscape not horror-film / a measured temple.  
Object: Āl-Yāhūdu contract.

**Daniel**  
Door: a palace night, a Judean at a window toward Jerusalem.  
Campfire: a furnace glow (no screaming) / a wall with writing / four beasts as carved relief, not CGI.  
Object: Jehoiachin ration tablet.

## The Twelve

**Hosea** — Door: a northern market of wine-jars. Campfire: a doorway left open / wilderness tenderness / a healed name. Object: Samaria ostracon.  
**Joel** — Door: a stripped field, sky copper. Campfire: locust on grain / elders at a porch / a poured-out spirit as rain, not cartoon fire. Object: silo.  
**Amos** — Door: ivory inlay on a rich table, a poor man at the edge of the frame. Campfire: a plumb line / summer fruit / a roaring from Zion as weather. Object: Samaria ivory.  
**Obadiah** — Door: an Edomite ridge looking toward a smoking city. Campfire: a brother at a crossroads / a nest in a cliff. Object: Edomite shrine stand.  
**Jonah** — Door: a harbor at Joppa, a ship pointed the wrong way. Campfire: a dark belly of timber / a city so large the road keeps going / a plant and an east wind. Object: Nineveh relief.  
**Micah** — Door: a small Shephelah town, smoke on the next ridge. Campfire: a court in the hills / swords beaten (as tools) / a woman in labor as hope not medical scene. Object: Halif burn-layer house.  
**Nahum** — Door: Nineveh’s walls from a distance, river and brick. Campfire: a scattered army on a relief / empty gates. Object: Babylonian chronicle tablet class.  
**Habakkuk** — Door: a watchtower, a man waiting. Campfire: violence on a road / a still field after. Object: 1QpHab column.  
**Zephaniah** — Door: Jerusalem streets being swept, silver on a throat. Campfire: a silent land / a singing remnant. Object: Ketef Hinnom amulet.  
**Haggai** — Door: a half-built platform, people in paneled houses behind. Campfire: work restarting / grain still thin. Object: Yehud seal.  
**Zechariah** — Door: night, a lampstand, a man among myrtle. Campfire: a flying scroll as image not toy / donkey-king on a humble road. Object: early second-temple stone.  
**Malachi** — Door: a worn altar, a province coin in a palm. Campfire: a marriage door / a scroll of remembrance. Object: Yehud coin.

## Gospels and Acts

**Matthew**  
Door: Magdala-class synagogue interior, carved stone, no celebrity Jesus.  
Campfire: a mountain teaching / bread in wilderness / a torn curtain implied by light.  
Object: Magdala Stone.

**Mark**  
Door: Capernaum courtyard, grindstone, pewter lake.  
Campfire: nets dropped / crowded doorway / road south / empty linen.  
Object: lead net-weight + Galilee boat type.

**Luke**  
Door: a small-town synagogue floor, mosaic edge, morning.  
Campfire: a manger that is a feeding trough / a Samaritan on a road / a table at Emmaus, faces not holy-cards.  
Object: mikveh steps + Magdala synagogue.

**John**  
Door: water jars at a wedding, night coming.  
Campfire: a well at noon / a tomb mouth / charcoal fire on a shore.  
Object: P52 + Pilate stone.

**Acts**  
Door: a rented upper room, wind in fabric, no tongues-of-fire clipart.  
Campfire: a Beautiful Gate / a road to Damascus as light not comic ray / a ship broken on Malta stones.

## Paul

**Romans** — Door: stacked Roman insula stair, mixed neighbors. Campfire: an olive tree / a collection bag / a woman on a road with a letter. Object: brick stamp.  
**1 Corinthians** — Door: shops against a temple precinct, meat on a stall. Campfire: one body as many hands at a table / a raised body implied by an empty wrapping. Object: market / precinct stones.  
**2 Corinthians** — Door: the Cenchreae road, a tired man, a letter. Campfire: cracked jar with light / a thorn as a plant, not gore. Object: milestone.  
**Galatians** — Door: a Galatian town street, an imperial inscription. Campfire: a broken yoke / fruit on a table. Object: Pisidian-class inscription.  
**Ephesians** — Door: terrace houses looking toward a vast precinct. Campfire: a wall coming down as masonry / armor as Roman kit used as metaphor, careful. Object: Artemis precinct stone.  
**Philippians** — Door: via Egnatia paving, Latin letters in a curb. Campfire: a jail at night / a hymn-shaped emptiness. Object: colony inscription.  
**Colossians** — Door: Lycus valley towns in a triangle of hills. Campfire: a household table / a name in a book. Object: regional civic stone.  
**1 Thessalonians** — Door: a forum with politarch-class civic stone. Campfire: work-worn hands / a coming as dawn, not a chart. Object: politarch inscription.  
**2 Thessalonians** — Door: same forum under rumor. Campfire: standing stones. Object: same civic stone.  
**1 Timothy** — Door: an Asian household doorway used as a church. Campfire: an elder’s hands / a young man and a scroll. Object: benefactor inscription.  
**2 Timothy** — Door: a dim rented room, cloak and scrolls. Campfire: winter sea / a chain on a table. Object: lamp + chain type.  
**Titus** — Door: a Cretan harbor town. Campfire: order in a household / a washing as water, not magic. Object: Gortyn-class civic stone.  
**Philemon** — Door: a modest peristyle, a man at the threshold with no papers. Campfire: a letter in two hands / a chain set down. Object: slave-sale tablet type.

**James** — Door: grindstone and loom weights, a gold ring in the same room. Campfire: a tongue as fire in a lamp not a cartoon / oil on a forehead. Object: grindstone.  
**1 Peter** — Door: a modest Asian threshold, a far cross-method in memory not gore. Campfire: living stones as a wall / a lion as threat at the edge of the frame. Object: Yehohanan-class nail *with warning, Summit only*. Path: the threshold.  
**2 Peter** — Door: a lamp in a dark street. Campfire: a mountain bright / a delayed fire as weather. Object: P72-class page.  
**1 John** — Door: hands and a table, light through a doorway. Campfire: oil / a closed throat that will not confess. Object: early Johannine papyrus.  
**2 John** — Door: a house door, a traveler. Campfire: a warning at the latch. Object: guest threshold.  
**3 John** — Door: the same door from inside. Campfire: a welcome stool / a refused stool. Object: same.  
**Jude** — Door: a desert copy of Enoch beside a short letter. Campfire: a feast / a stained garment. Object: 4QEnoch fragment.  
**Revelation** — Door: Patmos rock and water, seven lampstands as metal not clipart. Campfire: a door standing open in heaven as architecture / a city coming down as light, not a tourist map / Laodicea’s pipes. Object: Laodicea water system + imperial architecture.

## Tewahedo-distinct

**1 Enoch** — Door: a ridge under a hard sky, no Hollywood angels. Campfire: a bound watcher as silhouette / a tour of places as landscape. Object: 4Q201. No “forbidden book” thumbnail language.  
**Jubilees** — Door: Sinai, tablets, a 364-day ring as a simple calendar wheel. Object: 4QJubilees.  
**1–3 Meqabyan** — Door: a Ge’ez manuscript on a wooden rest, church light. Object: the manuscript — transmission, not a fake DSS.  
**4 Baruch** — Door: Jerusalem after fire, a hidden thing in a field. Object: destruction layer.
