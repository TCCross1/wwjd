# Path library — Major prophets

## Isaiah
**Door:** A man sees the Holy and is undone, then spends a lifetime saying a crushed people can be comforted.  
**Campfire:** Uzziah dies. Smoke fills the house. “Here I am.” Warnings to kings. A child-sign. Assyria like a razor. Then, after the midpoint, “Comfort, comfort.” A servant who suffers. A new heaven language.  
**Passport:** Tradition — Isaiah of Jerusalem for the whole. Other — Isaiah’s voice plus later disciples in exile and after (often chs. 40–55 and 56–66). Both chairs hear one book in church.  
**Object:** 1QIsaᵃ + Sennacherib prism.  
**Walkthrough:** Holy and wound (1–39) → Comfort (40–55) → Wide hope (56–66).  
**Chairs:** One prophet vs. a school. Do not mock either.  
**Thread:** Jesus reads Isaiah 61 in Nazareth. The servant songs are the church’s oldest Jesus-map.  
**Carry:** Comfort is spoken to people who have already been ruined.

## Jeremiah
**Door:** A young man told he was known before birth has to say the city will fall.  
**Campfire:** Call. Temple sermon. Yoke. Burned scroll written again. Cistern. Bought field. Letter to exiles: plant gardens. Then the end he promised.  
**Passport:** Jeremiah and Baruch. Dates in Josiah to after 586.  
**Object:** Lachish ostraca.  
**Walkthrough:** Call (1–10) → Fight with the city (11–29) → New covenant (30–33) → Fall (34–45) → Nations (46–52).  
**Chairs:** The new covenant as future Jesus vs. as return-from-exile first. Both belong in the room.  
**Thread:** Jesus is asked if He is Jeremiah. The cup and the new covenant sit at His table.  
**Carry:** Telling the truth can feel like betrayal and still be love.

## Ezekiel
**Door:** A priest with no temple sees glory on a canal.  
**Campfire:** Wheels. A scroll that tastes sweet and sits bitter. Street theater. The glory leaves. Dry bones. A measured new house. A river from the threshold.  
**Passport:** Ezekiel among the 597 exiles.  
**Object:** Āl-Yāhūdu contract. Does not prove he signed it.  
**Walkthrough:** Glory leaves (1–24) → Nations (25–32) → Bones and a heart (33–39) → House and river (40–48).  
**Chairs:** Vision-report vs. highly crafted priestly vision.  
**Thread:** John will steal this throne-room language. The river shows up again at the end of the Bible.  
**Carry:** God can leave a building and still find a people by a canal.

## Daniel
**Door:** Judean boys in a palace learn that kingdoms have numbers, and they are not first.  
**Campfire:** Diet. Furnace. Hand on a wall. Lions. Then dreams of beasts and a human figure given a kingdom. Seventy sevens. A man clothed in linen.  
**Passport:** Tradition — Daniel in exile, 6th century. Other — finished in the 160s BC under Antiochus, using exile tales. Qumran already has Daniel young.  
**Object:** Jehoiachin ration tablet + early Daniel copies.  
**Walkthrough:** Tales (1–6) → Visions (7–12).  
**Chairs:** 6th-century report vs. 2nd-century resistance literature. The book still says: do not bow.  
**Thread:** “Son of Man” is Jesus’ favorite name for Himself.  
**Carry:** The palace is not the last kingdom.
