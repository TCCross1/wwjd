# Path library — The Twelve

## Hosea
**Door:** A prophet’s marriage becomes a sermon: God will not quit a faithless people.  
**Campfire:** Take a wife of whoredom. Children with hard names. Then “I will heal their turning away.”  
**World:** Northern prosperity, Samaria jars. Object: Samaria ostraca.  
**Chairs:** Real marriage vs. enacted parable.  
**Thread:** “Out of Egypt I called my son” — Matthew hears Jesus.  
**Carry:** Love that will not let go is not weakness.

## Joel
**Door:** Locusts first. Spirit later.  
**Campfire:** The land is eaten. Rend your hearts. Afterward I will pour out my Spirit on all flesh.  
**Object:** Silo.  
**Thread:** Peter stands up at Pentecost with this book open.  
**Carry:** The worst field is not the last word.

## Amos
**Door:** A herdsman from the south walks into the north’s luxury and says the poor have a witness.  
**Campfire:** For three sins and four. Ivory houses. A plumb line. A basket of fruit.  
**Object:** Samaria ivories.  
**Thread:** James will quote Amos when the church opens the door to Gentiles.  
**Carry:** Worship that ignores the poor is noise.

## Obadiah
**Door:** A short shout at a brother who watched Jerusalem burn.  
**Campfire:** Edom stood aside. The kingdom will be the LORD’s.  
**Object:** Edomite shrine class.  
**Thread:** Brother-hate is not a small sin in this family.  
**Carry:** Gloating over a fall is remembered.

## Jonah
**Door:** A prophet runs west from a God who loves the enemy city.  
**Campfire:** Storm. Fish. Nineveh sits in dust. Jonah pouts under a plant. God asks about the city and the cattle.  
**Object:** Nineveh relief. Does not prove the fish.  
**Chairs:** Report vs. a holy satire that tells the truth.  
**Thread:** Jesus names Jonah as the sign — three days, then preach.  
**Carry:** Can I stand it if God is kind to them?

## Micah
**Door:** From a small town, a word against both capitals, and a promised ruler from Bethlehem.  
**Campfire:** Heads who eat the poor. Do justice, love mercy, walk humbly. A ruler from a little clan.  
**Object:** Halif burn layer.  
**Thread:** Matthew’s wise men get Bethlehem from this book.  
**Carry:** What the LORD requires is not hidden.

## Nahum
**Door:** The city Jonah pitied falls, and someone sings.  
**Campfire:** Nineveh’s end. The LORD is slow to anger and will not clear the guilty forever.  
**Object:** Chronicle of 612 BC.  
**Thread:** Empires are numbered. Revelation will use this kind of taunt.  
**Carry:** Comfort for the crushed can sound like a dirge to the palace.

## Habakkuk
**Door:** A prophet asks how long, and learns to wait on a watchtower.  
**Campfire:** Why do the wicked swallow the righteous? God is raising Babylon. That is worse. The just shall live by faith. Yet I will rejoice.  
**Object:** 1QpHab.  
**Thread:** Paul builds Romans and Galatians on that line.  
**Carry:** Rejoicing can be a decision in the dark.

## Zephaniah
**Door:** The Day is near, and after the sweeping there is singing.  
**Campfire:** Judah’s complacent. Nations too. Then “He will quiet you with His love.”  
**Object:** Ketef Hinnom from the same century-world.  
**Thread:** The remnant idea feeds the Gospels’ poor-in-spirit.  
**Carry:** The Day is not only fire. It is also a song.

## Haggai
**Door:** You live in paneled houses and the LORD’s house is a ruin.  
**Campfire:** Two months of sermons. Work restarts. The latter house will have greater glory.  
**Object:** Yehud seal.  
**Thread:** Greater glory — the church reads Jesus walking in a modest second temple.  
**Carry:** Start the work you postponed.

## Zechariah
**Door:** Night visions and a humble king on a donkey.  
**Campfire:** Horsemen, lampstand, flying scroll. Two olive trees. Thirty pieces. A fountain.  
**Object:** Early second-temple stone.  
**Thread:** Palm Sunday rides this donkey on purpose.  
**Carry:** The king you wanted may arrive small.

## Malachi
**Door:** A tired province asks “where is the God of justice?” and is told a messenger is coming.  
**Campfire:** Polluted offerings. Faithless marriage. Robbing God. A scroll of remembrance. Elijah before the Day.  
**Object:** Yehud coin.  
**Thread:** Gospels open with a messenger in Elijah’s coat.  
**Carry:** Weariness is not an excuse to give God leftovers.
