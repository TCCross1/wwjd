# Path library — Paul

## Romans
See `books/ROMANS.md` for gold. Path:

**Door:** A letter about how God makes enemies into a family without erasing Israel.  
**Campfire:** All have sinned. God justifies the ungodly by faith. Abraham. Adam and Christ. Baptized into a death. Spirit and adoption. Israel’s story not over. A living sacrifice. Phoebe carries it.  
**Object:** Insula stair.  
**Chairs:** Written 57-ish from Corinth — that dating is widely shared. The fight is how chapters 9–11 and “justification” sit.  
**Thread:** The cross here is not a mood. God puts the ungodly right.  
**Carry:** There is no boasting left. There is a mercy left.

## 1 Corinthians
**Door:** A gifted, messy church is told that love is a way, and a raised body is the hope.  
**Campfire:** Divisions. Lawsuits. Sex. Meat by temples. The table. Gifts. A hymn to love. Resurrection chapter.  
**Object:** Market against a precinct.  
**Thread:** Christ crucified, then raised.  
**Carry:** Gifts without love are noise.

## 2 Corinthians
**Door:** A wounded apostle defends a ministry that looks like dying.  
**Campfire:** Comfort. A severe letter. Treasure in clay jars. A thorn. Boasting in weakness.  
**Object:** Cenchreae road.  
**Thread:** The pattern is cross-shaped power.  
**Carry:** Weakness can be a place God stays.

## Galatians
**Door:** If you add a badge to Jesus, you have another gospel.  
**Campfire:** Paul is astonished. Abraham. Faith. Fruit of the Spirit. No east or west in this family.  
**Object:** Imperial town inscription.  
**Thread:** Christ lives in me.  
**Carry:** Freedom is not a new law with a Jesus sticker.

## Ephesians
**Door:** One new humanity, a wall down, armor for a long fight.  
**Campfire:** Chosen in Christ. Saved by grace. Jew and Gentile one. Household codes. Stand.  
**Object:** Ephesus precinct.  
**Chairs:** Paul in prison vs. a student in Paul’s voice. Both chairs are old in the church.  
**Carry:** Grace is not a feeling. It is how you got in.

## Philippians
**Door:** Joy from a jail, and a hymn about a Lord who went down.  
**Campfire:** Chains advance the gospel. He emptied Himself. Press on. A peace that guards.  
**Object:** Via Egnatia.  
**Thread:** The name above every name.  
**Carry:** Citizenship is in heaven, not in the colony’s pride.

## Colossians
**Door:** Christ is not one option on a spiritual menu.  
**Campfire:** He is the image. Through Him all things. Do not be captured by shadows.  
**Object:** Lycus valley.  
**Chairs:** Paul vs. a close student.  
**Carry:** You are complete in Him, not in extra stairs to heaven.

## 1 Thessalonians
**Door:** A young church waiting, working, and grieving with hope.  
**Campfire:** Faith, love, hope. A holy life. The dead in Christ. Encourage one another.  
**Object:** Politarch stone.  
**Carry:** Grief is allowed. Despair is not required.

## 2 Thessalonians
**Door:** Do not panic. The Day has not slipped past you.  
**Campfire:** Relief and judgment. A man of lawlessness. Keep working.  
**Chairs:** Same author vs. a later hand.  
**Carry:** Stand. Do not quit your job for a rumor.

## 1 Timothy
**Door:** How a household of God should behave while the truth is under weather.  
**Campfire:** False teachers. Prayer. Elders. A good confession.  
**Chairs:** Paul vs. a later pastor using Paul. The church has long read it as Scripture either way.  
**Carry:** Guard the good deposit.

## 2 Timothy
**Door:** Last letter weather. Come before winter.  
**Campfire:** Fan the gift. All Scripture is God-breathed. I have finished. Luke is with me.  
**Object:** Lamp and cloak.  
**Carry:** The work can outlive the worker.

## Titus
**Door:** Put what remains in order on a hard island.  
**Campfire:** Elders. Sound teaching. Grace that trains.  
**Carry:** Grace teaches; it does not shrug.

## Philemon
**Door:** A runaway’s name is now “useful,” and a master is asked to receive a brother.  
**Campfire:** Paul could command. He asks. Onesimus. Charge it to me.  
**Object:** Slave-sale tablet type. People were paper.  
**Thread:** The gospel gets into property law.  
**Carry:** Calling someone brother has a cost.
