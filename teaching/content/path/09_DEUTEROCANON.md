# Path library — Deuterocanon
Label by tradition pack. Never “fake books.”
Protestant default: “Read as Scripture in other churches.”
Catholic/Orthodox: these are Scripture on their shelf.

## Tobit
**Door:** A diaspora family, a grave at night, a journey, and God using a companion.  
**Campfire:** Tobit goes blind doing right. Tobias walks with Raphael unrecognised. A fish. A wedding. Eyes opened.  
**Object:** 4Q196–200. The story is older than a late church add-on.  
**Thread:** Angels, alms, and a God who sees in Nineveh’s shadow.  
**Carry:** Faithfulness in a foreign street still counts.

## Judith
**Door:** A widow walks into a general’s camp to save her town.  
**Campfire:** A siege. Judith prays, dresses, and ends the terror. A song.  
**Genre:** Deliverance tale. Not a war diary.  
**Carry:** Courage can look like a woman the officers underestimated.

## Wisdom of Solomon
**Door:** Wisdom is a spirit who loves humans, and idol-making is a tragic craft.  
**Campfire:** The souls of the righteous. Wisdom beside God. A critique of statues.  
**Object:** Stone cup vs. shop figurine.  
**Thread:** Language the church later used for Christ as Wisdom.  
**Carry:** What you bow to makes you.

## Sirach (Ecclesiasticus)
**Door:** A Jerusalem teacher writes a book his grandson will translate into Greek.  
**Campfire:** Fear of the Lord. Friends. Speech. A long praise of the fathers.  
**Object:** Hebrew Ben Sira at Masada and Qumran.  
**Carry:** Honor the wise who came before you.

## Baruch
**Door:** A letter of confession from the exile, and a poem telling Jerusalem to take off her mourning.  
**Campfire:** We have sinned. Wisdom is a gift. Look east, Jerusalem.  
**Thread:** Exile-hope that feeds later readers of the prophets.  
**Carry:** Confession is a road home.

## Letter of Jeremiah
**Door:** Do not fear the gods that are carried on shoulders.  
**Object:** 7Q2 Greek scrap at Qumran.  
**Carry:** A statue needs to be nailed down. God does not.

## Additions to Esther
**Door:** The Greek Esther prays and names God where the Hebrew book was silent.  
**Ink:** Two Esthers in the library. Tell both.  
**Carry:** Some churches could not stand a holy story with no prayer on the page.

## Additions to Daniel (Song of the Three, Susanna, Bel)
**Door:** Extra rooms in Daniel’s house: a song in fire, a woman cleared, idols exposed.  
**Ink:** Not in the Hebrew Daniel at Qumran as these Greek scenes. In the great Greek Bibles, yes.  
**Carry:** The church kept telling Daniel-stories because the furnace was not finished.

## 1 Maccabees
**Door:** A family fights so the altar can be clean again.  
**Campfire:** Antiochus. Mattathias. Judas. Dedication. Politics after the miracle.  
**Object:** Hasmonean coin.  
**Carry:** Freedom can start holy and turn into a throne.

## 2 Maccabees
**Door:** The same years, told as theology: martyrs, mercy, and a God who raises the dead.  
**Campfire:** Heliodorus. The mother and sons. Judas.  
**Hard Ground:** Path does not stage torture.  
**Thread:** Hebrews’ hall of faith remembers people who would not accept release.  
**Carry:** Resurrection hope is older than Easter morning in this chair.

## 1 Esdras
**Door:** A Greek retelling of the return, with a contest about what is strongest.  
**Carry:** Truth is strongest — a scene some churches kept.

## Prayer of Manasseh
**Door:** A wicked king is given a prayer of repentance.  
**Carry:** No one is beneath asking.

## Psalm 151
**Door:** A small shepherd’s psalm that the Greek and Qumran Bibles kept.  
**Object:** 11Q5.  
**Carry:** God took me from the flock.

## 3 Maccabees (Orthodox extra)
**Door:** Jews in Egypt under a Ptolemy, threatened and kept.  
**Carry:** Purim is not the only night God watched a people in a palace.
