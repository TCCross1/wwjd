# Path library — Gospels and Acts
Use gold files for Trail/Summit on Mark. Path here is enough to ship all four plus Acts.

## Matthew
**Door:** A Jewish Gospel that keeps saying “this was to fulfill,” and ends with a command to the nations.  
**Image:** Magdala-class synagogue stone.  
**Campfire:** A genealogy with unexpected women. A baby hunted by a king. Sermon on a mountain. Five teaching blocks. Peter’s confession. Passion week. An empty tomb and a mountain in Galilee: go, make disciples.  
**Passport:** Tradition — Matthew the tax collector. Other — a Jewish-Christian teacher using Mark plus sayings, often 70–90 AD.  
**Object:** Magdala Stone. Does not prove Jesus taught over it.  
**Walkthrough:** Born king (1–2) → Kingdom teaching (3–13) → Road to the cross (14–20) → Week and commission (21–28).  
**Chairs:** Apostle-author vs. a later school in his name.  
**Ink:** Early papyri; no original signature.  
**Thread:** This whole book *is* the Thread. Jesus is son of David, son of Abraham, God-with-us.  
**Carry:** The mountain at the end is still open.

## Mark
See `books/MARK.md` for full gold. Path short:

**Door:** Fast Gospel. Cross in the middle. Oldest copies stop before you are ready.  
**Campfire:** Water tears. Immediately. Nets drop. Three predictions. A Roman officer sees. Women run.  
**Object:** Net-weight. Not Andrew’s.  
**Ink:** 16:8 in Vaticanus and Sinaiticus; 16:9–20 beloved and later in most copies. The resurrection is already announced at the empty tomb.  
**Thread:** Do not use power the way the booth uses it.  
**Carry question:** What would I drop if He said my name at the boat?

## Luke
**Door:** An orderly account for someone named Theophilus, full of songs, meals, and people on the edges.  
**Campfire:** Two births. Mary’s song. A trough. Nazareth rejects Him. Samaritans, women, the lost son, a rich man and Lazarus. A long walk to Jerusalem. Two on a road to Emmaus who do not know Him until the bread.  
**Passport:** Tradition — Luke the physician, Paul’s friend. Other — a gifted Gentile-facing writer using Mark plus other memory, often 80–90 AD.  
**Object:** First-century synagogue + mikveh.  
**Walkthrough:** Infancy (1–2) → Galilee (3–9) → Road (10–19) → City and rising (20–24).  
**Chairs:** Companion of Paul vs. a later historian in the church.  
**Thread:** Jesus is the one who eats with the wrong people and opens Scripture on the road.  
**Carry:** He is known in the breaking of bread.

## John
**Door:** In the beginning was the Word, and the Word became flesh and camped among us.  
**Campfire:** Seven signs. Seven I AMs. A night talk with Nicodemus. A well at noon. A man born blind. Lazarus. A long table-talk. A side opened. A charcoal fire on a shore.  
**Passport:** Tradition — John the son of Zebedee. Other — a Johannine circle at the end of the first century.  
**Object:** P52 + Pilate stone.  
**Walkthrough:** Signs (1–12) → Hour (13–19) → Life (20–21).  
**Chairs:** Eyewitness apostle vs. a later beloved-disciple school.  
**Ink:** Woman caught in adultery is a beloved later insertion in most Bibles — tell it in Ink, do not sneer.  
**Thread:** This book exists so you may believe Jesus is the Christ and have life.  
**Carry:** Life is a Person, not a theory.

## Acts
**Door:** The risen Jesus leaves, the Spirit arrives, and the message walks from Jerusalem to Rome.  
**Campfire:** Wind. Tongues. Peter preaches. Stephen dies. Saul is knocked down. Gentiles receive the Spirit. Councils. Shipwreck. House-arrest. The last word is unhindered.  
**Passport:** Same author as Luke. Tradition — Luke. Date often 60s or 80s.  
**Object:** Gallio inscription dates Acts 18. Erastus stone may or may not be Romans 16.  
**Walkthrough:** Jerusalem (1–7) → Judea-Samaria (8–12) → Ends of the earth (13–28).  
**Chairs:** Companion’s diary vs. a crafted church history.  
**Thread:** Jesus keeps acting. The book never says the work is finished.  
**Carry:** The gospel is a person walking, not a poster staying.
