# Path library — Poetry and wisdom

## Job
**Door:** A good man loses everything and will not accept cheap answers.  
**Campfire:** Job is blameless. Disaster falls. Friends talk too long. Job demands a hearing. God answers from a whirlwind with a world too big for Job’s courtroom. Job puts his hand on his mouth. God restores. The friends are wrong.  
**Passport:** Tradition — ancient, even Mosaic. Other — a wisdom drama written later. Setting: the east (Uz). Language: Hebrew of a high, hard kind.  
**Object:** Edomite/east-world cult stand. Does not prove a historical Job’s furniture.  
**Walkthrough:** Ruin (1–2) → Arguments (3–37) → Whirlwind (38–42).  
**Chairs:** Report of a man vs. a holy play about suffering.  
**Thread:** A righteous sufferer who will not curse God. The New Testament names Job’s endurance. Jesus is the sufferer who can ask why and still trust.  
**Carry:** Presence is a better answer than a theory.

## Psalms
**Door:** A book of prayers for every weather of a life.  
**Campfire:** Songs of David and others. Praise, rage, fear, “how long,” thank you. Five books inside one book. Ends with everything that has breath.  
**Passport:** Many authors. David’s name on many. Collected for temple and after.  
**Object:** 11Q5 Great Psalms Scroll; Psalm 151 lives there.  
**Walkthrough:** Book I–V as seasons of a soul.  
**Chairs:** Every “of David” is David’s pen vs. a choir using a king’s name.  
**Thread:** Jesus prays these. The church prays these in His mouth.  
**Carry:** You are allowed to say the true thing to God.

## Proverbs
**Door:** Wisdom stands in the gate and calls fools home.  
**Campfire:** A father talks to a son. Lady Wisdom raises her voice. Short sayings about work, words, sex, money, friends. A woman of strength at the end.  
**Passport:** Tradition — Solomon and later Hezekiah’s men. Other — a wisdom school’s collection.  
**Object:** Ostracon.  
**Walkthrough:** Speeches (1–9) → Sayings (10–29) → Words of others (30–31).  
**Chairs:** Promises as guarantees vs. the grain of a well-lived world.  
**Thread:** Paul calls Christ the wisdom of God.  
**Carry:** Fear of the LORD is the first skill.

## Ecclesiastes
**Door:** A wise man turns the sun over in his hand and finds it heavy.  
**Campfire:** Vanity, vapor, breath. Work, wine, wisdom, death. Fear God anyway. Enjoy the portion.  
**Passport:** Tradition — Solomon. Other — a later Qoheleth using a royal voice. Qumran has a copy.  
**Object:** 4QQohelet.  
**Walkthrough:** Vapor (1–4) → Time (5–8) → Remember your maker (9–12).  
**Chairs:** Solomon on the throne vs. a later teacher in his coat.  
**Thread:** The New Testament does not cancel the ache. It puts a risen life under it.  
**Carry:** The gift of the day is still a gift.

## Song of Songs
**Door:** Desire that is not ashamed, and a love that seeks.  
**Campfire:** Two lovers. Vineyard. Night watch. “My beloved is mine.” Read as human love, as God’s love for His people, or as both.  
**Passport:** Tradition — Solomon. Other — wedding poetry gathered later. At Qumran.  
**Walkthrough:** Seeking (1–3) → Garden (4–6) → Seal (7–8).  
**Chairs:** Plain love poetry vs. allegory of God and Israel / Christ and church. The house allows both without smirking.  
**Thread:** Paul will call the church a bride. This book taught the language.  
**Carry:** Holy love has a body and a vow.

## Lamentations
**Door:** A city is opened like a sack, and someone still says His mercies are new.  
**Campfire:** Five poems. Acrostics that break. Mothers, princes, a prophet-voice sitting in ash. “Great is thy faithfulness” is born here, not on a mug.  
**Passport:** Tradition — Jeremiah. Other — a singer in 586’s generation.  
**Object:** Burn layer.  
**Walkthrough:** Five poems, five weathers of grief.  
**Thread:** Jesus weeps over this city. The church borrows these words in every ruin.  
**Carry:** You can tell God the city burned and still wait for morning.
