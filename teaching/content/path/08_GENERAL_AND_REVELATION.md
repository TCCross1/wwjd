# Path library — General letters and Revelation

## Hebrews
**Door:** Jesus is better than the steps that led to Him, so do not drift.  
**Campfire:** Better than angels, Moses, priests. A new covenant. A veil opened. A race. A cloud of witnesses.  
**Passport:** Author unknown. Early church guessed Barnabas, Apollos, others — not Paul in most careful reading.  
**Object:** Stone vessel and mikveh.  
**Thread:** The priest who is also the offering.  
**Carry:** Draw near.

## James
**Door:** Faith that does not work is dead on the floor.  
**Campfire:** Trials. Tongue. Widows. Rich and poor in the same assembly. Oil and prayer.  
**Passport:** Tradition — James the Lord’s brother.  
**Object:** Grindstone.  
**Thread:** A brother of Jesus who sounds like the Sermon on the Mount.  
**Carry:** Be doers.

## 1 Peter
**Door:** Exiles, live holy while the empire watches.  
**Campfire:** A living hope. Living stones. Slaves and wives in hard houses. Suffer as a Christian. A lion at the edge.  
**Object:** Path: a modest threshold. Summit may show the crucifixion nail with a warning.  
**Thread:** Christ suffered, leaving you an example.  
**Carry:** Hope has a body that was raised.

## 2 Peter
**Door:** Remember, and do not let mockers steal the promise.  
**Campfire:** Make your calling sure. False teachers. A delayed fire that is still coming.  
**Chairs:** Peter vs. a later letter in Peter’s name. Copied early (P72).  
**Carry:** Patience is not forgetfulness.

## 1 John
**Door:** God is light and love; testers of the spirits are in the house.  
**Campfire:** What we have touched. If we say we have no sin. Antichrists. Love one another.  
**Ink:** The later “three heavenly witnesses” line is not in the old Greek. Do not sneer at people who grew up with it. Tell the truth.  
**Carry:** Love is how you know you have passed out of death.

## 2 John
**Door:** A short warning at a doorway: do not take a false teacher in.  
**Carry:** Hospitality has a latch.

## 3 John
**Door:** Welcome the ones who go out for the Name. Do not copy the man who loves to be first.  
**Carry:** Who gets the bed tells the truth about a church.

## Jude
**Door:** Contend for the faith; some have slipped in.  
**Campfire:** Old examples. A quote from Enoch. Keep yourselves in love. He is able to keep you.  
**Object:** Qumran Enoch. Does not make all 108 chapters Protestant Scripture.  
**Carry:** Mercy on those who doubt.

## Revelation
See `books/REVELATION.md` for gold. Path:

**Door:** A vision for seven churches under Rome, and a Lamb who looks slain.  
**Campfire:** Letters. Throne. Seals, trumpets, bowls. A city called Babylon falls. A city called Jerusalem comes down.  
**Object:** Laodicea water, Ephesus cult, Patmos. Lukewarm is local.  
**Chairs:** Soon in John’s day vs. a long map of the end. Do not let either chair erase the seven towns.  
**Thread:** The slain Lamb is the one on the throne.  
**Carry:** Worship is how you stay human under an empire.
