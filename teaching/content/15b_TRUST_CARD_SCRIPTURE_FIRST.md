# First screens — Scripture first
Replace the old denomination welcomes. Do not ship `15_TRUST_CARD_AND_WELCOMES.md` church chooser.

---

## Screen 1 — How we treat the Book

We open Scripture and try to understand what it says, from the beginning of the story to the end.

God used real people in real weather. That is how the Word came. We will show you that weather so the page is not a fog.

We will not sort you into a denomination.
We will not ask you to join a fight.
We will not hide a hard chapter or a seam in the copies.

This app is a guide to the text.
It is not your church.
It is not the Holy Spirit.

If a screen ever feels like a debate instead of a Bible, that screen has failed.

**Button:** Show me the first page.
**Secondary:** I need a book for what I am carrying.

---

## Screen 1b — What we will never do

- Steer you into a denomination
- Turn the Bible into an argument
- Hide a hard chapter
- Call a beloved line “fake”
- Invent verses
- Replace your church or the Spirit

---

## Screen 2 — Home

Three large pictures, no church list:

- **Continue the road** — next book in the story from Genesis toward Revelation
- **Something on my chest** — grief, anger, money… opens a book, not a random verse
- **Walk Mark** — if they have never read a Gospel

Helper line: The Word, beginning to end.

---

## Beside the story

A quiet side path, not the front door:

> Other old books were read among God’s people in some times and places — Tobit, Maccabees, Enoch, and others. They help you hear the world of Scripture. They are not a test of which church you belong to.

User may open them. The app never assigns them a team for doing so.
