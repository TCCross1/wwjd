# Visual house and Watch door
## Pictures first. Words second. Other people’s films on a side wall.

Many people will never finish a Campfire paragraph.
They will remember a courtyard at dawn, a net-weight in a palm, a map that moves.

If the House is only text, we built a seminary.
If the House is only video, we built a YouTube playlist with our logo on it.
The House owns the stills. Neighbors may hang a film on the Watch wall.

---

## Locked visual language

One look across every book. If Genesis looks like a tract and Mark looks like a graphic novel, the house falls apart.

**The stills are:**
- cinematic, quiet, lit like late afternoon or lamp-light
- bodies and objects first — hands, bread, boats, brick, doors — not posed holy cards
- historically careful: four-room houses, basalt Galilee, brick molds, canals
- no glowing European Jesus as the Door image
- no cute animals wearing robes
- no photoreal “photograph of the crucifixion”
- Path images may be warmer. Summit images may be closer to dirt and stone.

**Color weather by era (reuse, do not invent a new palette per book):**
- Ancestor road — dust, tent-cloth, well-water
- Egypt — Nile green against kiln-white and mud
- Hill village — limestone, olive, lamp-oil
- Exile — canal-brown, clay, date-gold
- Galilee — basalt black, lake-silver, courtyard dust
- Rome / letters — brick, bronze coin, workshop wood
- Vision — gold leaf against night, never a comic-book explosion

**Type on images:** almost none. If a word must sit on a picture, eight words or fewer.

## What each room is allowed to show

| Room | Visual | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Door | one still of that book’s world | the first thing the eye hits, before the sentence |
| Campfire | 3–5 stills that turn as the story is told | tap-to-advance, not a paragraph stack |
| Passport | no portrait of “the author” we cannot identify | two columns, clean type |
| World | dawn-to-dark strip + object photo | the object is a real class of find when we can get a museum image |
| Map | drawn map, then/now toggle | pins only for places the book names |
| Walkthrough | one still per movement | title on the image, chapter range under it |
| People | simple figures, not celebrity faces | unnamed woman and child must appear as often as kings |
| Two Chairs | two empty chairs at one table | never two angry men pointing |
| Ink | photo of a real page or fragment | P52, 1QIsaa, Ketef Hinnom, a Galilee boat — labeled |
| Hard Ground | shadow, not gore | Path gets a closed door or an empty road |
| Thread | one still that is *this* book’s Jesus-sight | Mark: a road and a cross-shaped absence, not a bracelet |
| Carry | the object they picked, large | share card uses that still |

A visual learner can walk Path by pictures alone if every still has a one-line caption. Captions are not optional. They are the book for someone who will not read the Campfire.

## How we get the pictures

Three sources, in this order. Never a fourth.

1. **Our stills** — generated or commissioned to the style lock above, from the World Engine and object trays we already wrote. These are the House’s face.
2. **Museum and excavation photos** with a clear license (Met, British Museum, Israel Museum, IAA releases, Wikimedia when the license is clean). Badge: `from the ground`.
3. **Simple drawn maps and house plans** we own.

Never:
- scrape a painting and crop the watermark
- use The Chosen stills without a license
- use a BibleProject frame as if we drew it
- let an image generator invent Hebrew letters or a “Dead Sea Scroll” that is not a real scroll
- put a modern actor’s face on Peter

If a generated still starts looking like a movie poster of Jesus, throw it away.

## Watch door — other people’s films

A button on the Door and after Campfire:

**Watch a neighbor tell this book (6–12 min)**

Rules:
- Never autoplay.
- Never the first thing in the house. Picture and one sentence first.
- Always labeled **a neighbor’s film, not our Thread.**
- User can skip. Skipping is not a lesser visit.
- Offline Path still works if YouTube is blocked.
- Tradition pack can swap the default neighbor (see below).
- If a video disappears, the house does not break. The stills stay.

We do not host their file. We open their page or an official embed. We do not download and re-upload. That is how you get loved and then sued.

### Second seat, when we want Jesus-on-every-page more explicit
**Spoken Gospel — “The Bible Explained.”**
Shorter, more homiletical, every book aimed at seeing Christ. Use as an *optional* second film, not the default in Jewish-sensitive or “arriving” packs — it jumps to the Thread faster than our house allows.

### Third ear
**Streetlights x BibleProject remixes** where they exist.
Same pictures, different cadence. Good when the user needs to *hear* the book, not only watch shapes.

### Catholic pack
Keep BibleProject as the picture-overview if it exists.
Add a second card: Fr. Mike Schmitz / Jeff Cavins *Bible in a Year* intro for that period — audio-forward, not a cartoon, but it is the film many Catholic sharers already trust.
Never tell a Catholic the deuterocanon has no Watch door. If no good film exists, say so and show our stills. Do not invent a cartoon of Tobit that treats it as a fable.

### Tewahedo pack
English film for 1 Enoch / Jubilees / Meqabyan is thin and often hostile or sensational.
Watch door copy:
> We will not send you a video that treats your books as a conspiracy. Our stills and Campfire are the path until a reverent film exists.

Do not link “forbidden books of Enoch” clickbait. That wing failed if we do.

### What never goes on the wall
- End-times countdown channels
- “They hid this book” thumbnails
- AI slop reciting Wikipedia over stock footage
- Debate-club takedowns of the book you are standing in
- The Chosen as if it were a Gospel (different art, different license, different job)

## Visual Path — Mark, as the pattern

1. **Door still:** basalt courtyard, grindstone, lake the color of pewter. No face of Jesus yet.
2. **Campfire stills:** water tearing / nets dropped / a crowded doorway / a road going south / a Roman-looking hill and an empty linen.
3. **World:** dawn-to-dark strip of five frames. Object: photograph of a lead net-weight (museum class).
4. **Watch (optional):** BibleProject *Mark*, labeled neighbor.
5. **Walkthrough:** five stills, one per movement.
6. **Chairs:** two chairs, one table, lamp.
7. **Ink:** a photograph of a fourth-century last page of Mark, caption about 16:8.
8. **Thread:** the road, not a slogan.
9. **Carry:** the net-weight, large.

A person who never reads a paragraph still got a gospel.

## Accessibility (this is part of visual, not an extra)

- Every still has a caption a screen reader can speak.
- Watch films have captions on. If they do not, we do not feature them.
- Do not put meaning only in color.
- Motion is tap-to-advance, not a loop that cannot be stopped.
- Data warning before a film on cellular.
