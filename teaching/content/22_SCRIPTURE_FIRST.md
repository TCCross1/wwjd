# Scripture first
## The one principle everything else hangs on

This feature is not a tour of churches.
It is not a debate club.
It is not a sorter that sends people into Protestant, Catholic, Orthodox, or Tewahedo rooms.

It is a walk through the Word — the text — from the first sentence to the last, so a person can understand what is written and meet Jesus in it.

Every other idea (pictures, daily life, old copies, maps, the WWJD chat) is a servant of that walk.
If an idea starts steering someone toward a denomination, it is rewritten or cut.

---

## The principle

**Open the book. Read it in order. Explain what the words are doing. Do not recruit.**

Branches from that:

1. The main road is Scripture from beginning to end.
2. We teach the text that is on the page, in plain speech.
3. We show the world the words came from so the page stops floating.
4. We show an old copy when it helps you trust what you are holding.
5. We name Jesus where the writing itself leads there — at the end of a book visit, not as a sticker on every line.
6. We do not ask “which church are you?” as the price of entry.
7. We do not stage fights. Where Christians have long heard a passage two ways, we say so in one calm sentence and return to the words.
8. Extra ancient books (Enoch, Tobit, Maccabees, and the rest) are part of *knowing the library the first readers lived beside*. They are not a membership test.

## What this changes in the app

### First screens
Delete the chooser:
- Protestant / evangelical
- Catholic
- Orthodox
- Ethiopian Tewahedo
- I do not want to say

Replace with:

**How we treat the Book**
Then:
**Start at the beginning** or **I need a book for what I am carrying** or **Walk Mark** (if they have never read a Gospel).

No flags. No church logos. No “your shelf vs their shelf.”

### The road
The default library is the story as one road:

Creation → promise → Egypt → land → kings → exile → return → the silence between → Jesus → the churches → the hope at the end.

A user can walk book by book in that order.
That is “beginning to end.”
That is the feature.

### “All of it”
Do not skip Leviticus, Judges, or the hard prophets to keep the app sweet.
Do not skip manuscript seams to keep the app safe.
Do not hide that other old books sat next to the Bible in the first centuries.

Present those extra books as:

> These writings were read among God’s people in some times and places. They help you hear the world of the text. They are not a second church.

Never: “your denomination includes this.”
Never: “they took this out to trick you.”

### Two Chairs become One Table
Keep the honesty. Lose the courtroom.

Old: Chair A vs Chair B, pick a team.
New: **What the words say. What people have wondered.** Then back to the words.

Example, Mark’s ending:
> The oldest big copies stop at the empty tomb and the women running. Most Bibles you buy also print the next twelve verses, which the church has loved for a long time. The risen Jesus is already announced before that debate starts. Keep reading.

No vote. No “liberals” vs “conservatives.” No “Rome vs Reformers.”

### Passport
Keep author and date as *help for hearing*, not as a trial.

> People have long received this as [traditional name]. Others, reading the same pages, have asked whether more than one hand shaped it. Either way, this is the book in front of you. Here is what it does.

Then teach the book.

### WWJD chat
Still gated on books they have entered.
It answers from the text they just walked, not from a denomination’s talking points.
It does not say “Baptists would tell you…” or “the Catholic Church teaches…”
It says: *in this book, Jesus does this.*

### Pictures, streets, objects, Watch door
Keep all of it.
BibleProject on the wall stays a neighbor film, not “our church.”
Captions stay about the story, not about a team.

## Trust Card — replacement text

**Title:** How we treat the Book

We open Scripture and try to understand what it says, from the beginning of the story to the end.

God used real people in real weather. That is how the Word came. We will show you that weather so the page is not a fog.

We will not sort you into a denomination.
We will not ask you to join a fight.
We will not hide a hard chapter or a seam in the copies.

This app is a guide to the text.
It is not your church.
It is not the Holy Spirit.

If a screen ever feels like a debate instead of a Bible, that screen has failed.

**Button:** Show me the first page.
**Secondary:** I need a book for what I am carrying.
