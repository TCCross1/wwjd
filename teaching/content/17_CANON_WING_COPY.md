## Three chairs, Path length

**Chair: the Hebrew shelf.**
The synagogue’s Bible is the Protestant Old Testament count. Jesus and the apostles lived in that world of books. Many Reformers said Christian doctrine should be built there.

**Chair: the church’s wider Greek Bible.**
The first centuries of the church read the Septuagint. Catholic and Orthodox Christians received more of those books as Scripture. They were not invented in 1546.

**Chair: a still wider African house.**
Ethiopia kept Enoch and Jubilees in the life of the church. Pieces of Enoch and Jubilees also sat in the dirt at Qumran. That does not automatically make them Protestant Scripture. It does mean they are not a late surprise.

You may live in one chair.
You may not kick the other two out of the house.
