# Jubilees
**Shelf:** Tewahedo Scripture · Hebrew rewritten Genesis–Exodus  
**Genre:** Covenant retelling on a calendar  
**Badge:** Tewahedo

---

## 1. The Door

**One sentence:** An angel dictates Genesis and part of Exodus to Moses again — this time with dates, feast laws, and a warning not to live by a wrong calendar.

**Time ribbon:** Mid-2nd century BC. Hebrew at Qumran. Whole book in Ge’ez.

**Path line:** “The same stories as Genesis, told as if heaven is keeping a strict clock.”

---

## 2. The Campfire

On Sinai, Moses is told more than the ten words. An angel of the presence opens heavenly tablets and walks him from creation to the Passover night, measuring time in weeks of years and jubilees (49-year blocks). Adam, Noah, Abraham, Jacob are still themselves — and more regulated. Angels keep Sabbath. Israel’s feasts are written into creation week. Marrying outside, eating blood, and following the nations’ calendar are treated as life-or-death.

It is Genesis with a priest’s ruler laid across it.

---

## 3. The Passport

**Claim inside the book:** revelation to Moses on Sinai.  
**Historical writing:** a Jewish author, almost certainly in Hebrew, around 160–150 BC (range argued, center of gravity in the Maccabean age).

**Audience:** Jews fighting to stay distinct under Greek pressure — the same generation that produced 1 Maccabees and parts of Daniel.

**Language path:** Hebrew (Qumran, ~14 copies) → lost Greek → Latin fragments → **complete Ge’ez**.

**Purpose:** To bind Israel’s story to a 364-day solar calendar and to a holiness that starts at creation, not at Sinai only.

---

## 4. Two Chairs — kept or set aside

**Chair A — Tewahedo, and Qumran before them.**  
This book was copied more at Qumran than Samuel or Kings. The Damascus Document treats it as authority. A church that received it with the Law is reading a Second Temple voice the desert monks also honored.

**Chair B — almost every other canon.**  
Josephus’ 22 do not include it. It is 2nd-century BC, not Mosaic ink. It rewrites Torah with extra rules. Western and Eastern councils never listed it.

**Shared ground:** It is Hebrew, old, and serious. It is one of the best windows we have into how some Jews read Genesis 150 years before Jesus.

---

## 5. Oldest copy

Hebrew fragments 4Q216–224, 11Q12, 1Q17–18, 2Q19–20, 3Q5 — 2nd–1st c. BC.
Ge’ez is how you read the whole argument.
The Ethiopic text, where it can be checked, follows the Hebrew closely. That is the opposite of “Ethiopia made this up.”

---

## 6. Thread to Jesus

Jubilees is not in Jesus’ quoted sentences the way Isaiah is. It shows the world He was born into: calendar fights, angelology, a Genesis that was already being preached as law.
When the Gospels argue about Sabbath and feast, they are walking in air Jubilees helped thicken.

**Remember**
1. Jubilees is Genesis retold, not Genesis replaced.
2. Qumran had the Hebrew. Ethiopia kept the book.
3. Calendar is the hill this book is willing to die on.
